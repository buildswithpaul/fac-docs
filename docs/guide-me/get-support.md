# How to get help and track your tickets

Raise a question or a bug from inside FAC Chat, then follow it and reply to support without leaving the app.

**You need:** FAC Chat and an up-to-date FAC Cloud.

## 1. Open Help & Feedback

Click the **?** button at the top right of FAC Chat. On **Report an issue**, write a short subject, pick a category (**Bug**, **Issue** or **Question**) and describe what you need. You can attach up to five images or PDFs.

![The Help & Feedback form filled in](/img/guide-me/support-form.webp)

**What we'll attach** shows the technical details sent with the ticket, such as app versions and your browser, so support does not have to ask for them.

## 2. Submit

Click **Submit**. You get a ticket number straight away, and **Track your ticket** takes you to it.

![The confirmation with the ticket number](/img/guide-me/support-submitted.webp)

## 3. See all your tickets

Open **Settings → Support**. Every ticket you raised is listed with its status. Filter by **Open**, **Replied**, **Resolved** or **Closed**.

![The list of tickets in Settings](/img/guide-me/support-list.webp)

## 4. Read the reply and answer it

Click a ticket to read the conversation. Support's replies appear here. Type in the box at the bottom, attach a file if it helps, and click **Send reply**.

![A ticket with a reply from support](/img/guide-me/support-thread.webp)

## 5. Send feedback without raising a ticket

In the same dialog, **Give feedback** takes a star rating, a category and a comment.

![The Give feedback tab](/img/guide-me/support-feedback.webp)

## Use cases

Each of these was done on the demo site.

| You want to | Do this | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Ask how to do something | **Report an issue**, category **Question**: "How do I share a page with my sales team?" | A ticket was created and showed as **Open** in Settings → Support |
| Follow up on a reply | Open the ticket and use **Send reply** | The reply and its attachment joined the same conversation |
| Report an answer that failed | Click **Report this** under the failed answer | The form opened with the error already filled in. See [Rate an answer or report a failed one](./rate-answers) |
