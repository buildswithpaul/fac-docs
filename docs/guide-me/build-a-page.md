# How to build a page by asking

A FAC Page is a full-screen page inside ERPNext with live figures, charts and sections your teams fill in. You describe it and FACO builds it. Here, a fortnightly review page for a management meeting.

**You need:** FAC Chat and an up-to-date FAC Cloud. Sharing a page with roles needs the **System Manager** role.

## 1. Describe the page

Open the chat widget on any Desk screen, or FAC Chat itself, and say what the page should show, who fills in what, and who should see it.

![Describing the page in the chat widget](/img/guide-me/page-ask.webp)

## 2. Approve the plan

FACO replies with one **Build plan**: every record type, rule and page it will create, in plain words. Nothing is built until you approve it.

![The build plan](/img/guide-me/page-plan.webp)

## 3. Publish it

FACO builds the page as a draft and shows it beside the chat. When you say "publish it", one card lists every change that goes live for other people, each with its code one click away.

![The go-live card](/img/guide-me/page-go-live.webp)

After you approve, the page is live for the roles you named. FACO says what it has and has not checked.

![The published page beside the chat](/img/guide-me/page-published.webp)

## 4. Let each team fill in its part

A team member opens the page from **Pages** in Desk. They see only what their ERPNext roles allow: here, a regional lead without access to the Profit and Loss report sees no profit figures. Their own section has an **Edit** button.

![A team member editing their section](/img/guide-me/page-team-edit.webp)

They type, click **Save**, and the page shows it for everyone.

![The saved section](/img/guide-me/page-team-saved.webp)

## 5. Present it

Click **Present**. The page fills the screen one section at a time. Arrow keys move between sections and **Esc** exits.

![Present mode](/img/guide-me/page-present.webp)

## 6. Find your pages later

**Pages** in Desk lists every page you can open, with a preview. Filter by **Mine** or **Shared**, search, and use a card's menu to edit it with FACO, rename it or delete it.

![The Pages gallery in Desk](/img/guide-me/page-gallery.webp)

## Use cases

Both pages below were built on the demo site by asking, as shown above.

| Ask for | What was built |
|---|---|
| "Build our Fortnightly Review page for the management meeting", with live P&L and receivables at the top and a section each region fills in | A shared page with four live figures and a section per region. Each regional lead could edit only their own section, and a lead without access to the P&L saw no profit figures |
| "Make me a page with this financial year's profit and loss by month" | A personal page with income, expense and net profit cards and a monthly chart, read from the Profit and Loss Statement |

Other pages that fit the same pattern: a sales summary your team checks each morning, receivables by customer for the Monday meeting, or a stock report you download as PDF.
