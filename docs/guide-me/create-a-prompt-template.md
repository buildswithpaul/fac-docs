# How to save your own prompt template by asking

When you find yourself asking the same careful question again and again, save it as a template with blanks. Ask FACO to save it; you do not fill in a form.

**You need:** FAC Chat and an up-to-date FAC Cloud.

## 1. Say what the template should ask, and what the blanks are

> Save a prompt template called "Customer statement" with one blank, the customer name. It should list that customer's unpaid sales invoices with due dates and days overdue, and total what they owe.

## 2. Approve the card

FACO checks that no similar template exists, then shows the new one on an approval card with its blanks listed under **Arguments**. Click **Approve**.

![The approval card for a new prompt template](/img/guide-me/newtemplate-card.webp)

## 3. Read the saved wording

FACO shows the full instruction it saved, with the blank marked, and says who can see it. It starts private to you; ask FACO to share it with your team when you are ready.

![FACO confirming the saved template](/img/guide-me/newtemplate-saved.webp)

## 4. Use it

Type **/** in the message box. Your template is in the list.

![The new template in the slash menu](/img/guide-me/newtemplate-slash.webp)

Fill in the blank and click **Use**.

![Filling in the customer name](/img/guide-me/newtemplate-params.webp)

![The statement for one customer](/img/guide-me/newtemplate-answer.webp)

## Use cases

Each of these was done on the demo site.

| You ask | What happened |
|---|---|
| The "Customer statement" request above | One approval card, then the template was saved. It cost 44 credits |
| The template, with *Blue Harbor Cafés* as the customer | A statement of five unpaid invoices with due dates and days overdue, and the total owed. It cost 26 credits |

Other questions worth saving: a monthly sales review with the month as a blank, or your team's standard stock check for a warehouse you name.
