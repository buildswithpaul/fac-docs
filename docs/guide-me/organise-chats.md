# Guide me: organise your chats

Find an older chat, give it a clear name, put it in the right project, and archive what you are done with.

## 1. Search

Type in **Search chats** at the top of the sidebar. The list narrows as you type.

![Searching chats for "Blue Harbor"](/img/guide-me/chats-search.webp)

## 2. Rename

Hover over the chat, click **⋯** and choose **Rename**. Type the new name and press **Enter**.

![Renaming a chat in the sidebar](/img/guide-me/chats-rename.webp)

You can also rename the chat you have open by clicking its title at the top of the page.

## 3. Move it into a project

Click **⋯** again, choose **Move to project** and pick the project. You can also start a **New project…** from here, or remove the chat from its project.

![Moving a chat into a project](/img/guide-me/chats-move.webp)

A chat in a project shows a dot in the sidebar, and it is listed on the project page right away.

## 4. Archive what you are done with

Click **⋯**, choose **Archive** and confirm with ✓. The chat leaves the list, and you stay on the page you were on.

![Confirming an archive](/img/guide-me/chats-archive.webp)

Archived chats are not deleted. Open **Archived** at the bottom of the chat list to see them or carry on with one.

## The result

The project page lists every chat for the deal in one place.

![The project page listing both chats](/img/guide-me/chats-in-project.webp)
