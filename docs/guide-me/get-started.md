# How to get started with FAC Chat

From a fresh install to your first answer. You do not create an API key, add a provider or pick a model: you confirm one email address and FAC Cloud does the rest.

**You need:** the FAC app installed on your site ([installation](/getting-started/installation)) and the **System Manager** role. If your site runs on your own computer, read [Local & private sites](/fac-chat/local-sites) first.

## 1. Switch on FAC Chat

After the install, Desk shows a banner at the top. Click **Switch on FAC Chat**. No restart is needed.

![The Switch on FAC Chat banner in Desk](/img/guide-me/start-banner.webp)

If you dismissed the banner, switch it on in **Assistant Core Settings → FAC Chat** instead.

## 2. Enter the owner's email

FAC Chat opens at `/copilot` with your email already filled in. This address owns the workspace and receives the verification link. Click **Get Started**.

![The welcome screen with the owner email](/img/guide-me/start-welcome.webp)

## 3. Accept the terms

Read the terms, tick the box and click **Accept & Register**.

![The terms dialog](/img/guide-me/start-terms.webp)

## 4. Confirm your email

FAC Chat waits while you check your inbox.

![FAC Chat waiting for the email to be confirmed](/img/guide-me/start-check-email.webp)

The email names the site you are connecting. Stay signed in to that site in the same browser, then click **Confirm registration**. The link works for 24 hours.

![The confirmation email](/img/guide-me/start-email.webp)

## 5. Connect your account

Your site is now registered. Click **Connect Account** so FACO works as you: it reads what you can read and changes nothing without your approval. Every user who opens FAC Chat does this step once.

![The Connect your account screen](/img/guide-me/start-connect.webp)

## 6. Finish the short setup

A short intro plays, which you can skip. You start on the **Free plan** with no card.

![The Free plan step](/img/guide-me/start-plan.webp)

Tell FACO your job title and department if you like, so it answers in your context. The last step asks whether FACO may remember things from your conversations; it stays off unless you switch it on.

![The About You step](/img/guide-me/start-about-you.webp)

## 7. Ask your first question

Click **Get Started**. A four-step tour points out where things are; **Skip tour** closes it.

![The FAC Chat home page with the first-use tour](/img/guide-me/start-home.webp)

Type a question in plain words and press **Enter**. Here, a question about the site's users on a brand-new site.

![The first answer](/img/guide-me/start-answer.webp)

The line under the answer shows the credits it used, and the bar at the top shows how much of your month's allowance is gone.

## Use cases

Good first questions. Each one was asked on a demo site and answered from live data.

| Ask | What you get |
|---|---|
| "Which users are on this site, and what roles does each one have?" | A table of users and roles. This is the answer in the screenshot above, from a brand-new site |
| "How many customers do we have, and which customer group has the most?" | A count and the largest group |
| "Which 5 customers have the highest outstanding receivables right now?" | A ranked table with amounts |
| "How many sales invoices were submitted this year and what is their total?" | The count and the total |
| "What are the mandatory fields I need to fill to create a Sales Order?" | The required fields, read from your site's own setup |
