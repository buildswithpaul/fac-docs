# How to see and manage what FACO remembers

With memory on, FACO learns your preferences and standing facts from your conversations, so you do not repeat yourself. You can read, correct, share or delete every item.

**You need:** FAC Chat, an up-to-date FAC Cloud, and memory switched on for your account.

## 1. Switch memory on or off

Open **Settings → Privacy & Data**. **AI Memory** is your own choice and starts off unless your administrator set it otherwise. **Restrict data processing** pauses memory without deleting anything.

![The privacy settings](/img/guide-me/memory-privacy.webp)

The same page lets you download your data, archive every conversation, or delete everything.

## 2. Read the summary

Open **Settings → Memory**. **Summary** is a short profile written from your memories: your work, how you like answers, and key milestones. **Regenerate** rewrites it.

![The memory summary](/img/guide-me/memory-summary.webp)

## 3. Manage single memories

Click **Memories** to see each item, grouped into how you work and things you have told FACO. Hover over an item for three actions: **Edit memory** to correct it, **Share to team** to make it available to everyone in your workspace, and **Delete memory**.

![The list of memories](/img/guide-me/memory-list.webp)

**Share to team** asks you to confirm before anything is shared.

![Confirming Share to team](/img/guide-me/memory-share.webp)

**Clear all memories** at the bottom removes everything outside projects.

## Use cases

| You want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| Stop repeating how you like answers | Tell FACO once ("always show amounts in lakh, as a table"), then check it appears under **Memories** |
| Fix something FACO got wrong about you | **Edit memory** on the item |
| Give the whole team a fact only you told FACO | **Share to team** on the item |
| Work on something sensitive | Switch on **Restrict data processing** first |
