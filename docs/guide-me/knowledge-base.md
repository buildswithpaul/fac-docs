# Guide me: answer from your own documents

Upload price lists, policies or procedures to the Knowledge Base. FACO answers from them and shows the page it used. Team Notes hold short facts everyone's FACO should know.

**You need:** FAC Chat, an up-to-date FAC Cloud and a plan that includes the Knowledge Base.

## 1. Upload a document

Open **Knowledge** in the left rail and click **Upload**. PDF, Markdown and text files work. A document shows **Processing**, then **Ready** once FACO can search it.

![The Knowledge Base with one document](/img/guide-me/knowledge-list.webp)

## 2. Choose who can search it

Open a document's menu and click **Manage access**. **Public** lets everyone in your workspace search it, **Private** keeps it to you, and **Shared** lets you pick the people.

![The Manage Access dialog](/img/guide-me/knowledge-access.webp)

## 3. Add team notes

**Team Notes** is one shared note for the whole workspace, written in Markdown. Use it for standing facts: payment terms, who signs off what, when the books close. Click **Add Notes**, write, and click **Save & Embed**.

![Writing team notes](/img/guide-me/knowledge-notes.webp)

## 4. Ask

Ask in any chat, in plain words. FACO searches the documents you are allowed to see and names the document and page it answered from.

## Use cases

| Upload | Then ask |
|---|---|
| A travel and expense policy | "What does our travel policy say about hotel limits?" |
| A price list | "What is our price for this item?" |
| A returns procedure | "Which steps does our returns SOP list?" |
| Team notes with your credit terms | "What are our standard payment terms?" |

**More:** [Start a project](./start-a-project) keeps documents with one piece of work instead of the whole workspace.
