# Guide me: answer from your own documents

Upload price lists, policies or procedures to the Knowledge Base. FACO answers from them and shows the page it used. Team Notes hold short facts everyone's FACO should know.

**You need:** FAC Chat, an up-to-date FAC Cloud and a plan that includes the Knowledge Base.

## 1. Upload a document

Open **Knowledge** in the left rail and click **Upload**. PDF, Markdown and text files work. Choose who can search the document before you confirm.

![Choosing visibility while uploading](/img/guide-me/knowledge-upload.webp)

The document shows **Processing**, then **Ready** once FACO can search it. A short file is ready in a few seconds.

![The Knowledge Base with two documents ready](/img/guide-me/knowledge-uploaded.webp)

## 2. Ask

Ask in any chat, in plain words. FACO searches the documents you are allowed to see, answers, and lists the document under **Sources**.

![An answer from an uploaded policy, with its source](/img/guide-me/knowledge-answer.webp)

## 3. Change who can search a document

Open a document's menu and click **Manage access**. **Public** lets everyone in your workspace search it, **Private** keeps it to you, and **Shared** lets you pick the people.

![The Manage Access dialog](/img/guide-me/knowledge-access.webp)

## 4. Add team notes

**Team Notes** is one shared note for the whole workspace, written in Markdown. Use it for standing facts: payment terms, who signs off what, when the books close. Click **Add Notes**, write, and click **Save & Embed**.

![Writing team notes](/img/guide-me/knowledge-notes.webp)

The note shows **Indexed** when it is ready.

![Team notes saved and indexed](/img/guide-me/knowledge-notes-saved.webp)

Everyone's FACO can now answer from it.

![An answer from the team notes](/img/guide-me/knowledge-notes-answer.webp)

## Use cases

Each of these was done on the demo site.

| Uploaded or written | Asked | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| A travel and expense policy (PDF) | "What does our travel and expense policy say about hotel limits?" | A table of nightly limits by city tier, the approval rule, and the policy named as the source. It cost 61 credits |
| A returns procedure (Markdown) | "Which steps does our returns SOP list, and is there a restocking fee?" | The five steps with owners and timings, and the 10% fee with its exception |
| Team notes with credit terms and the month-end date | "What are our standard payment terms, and when do the books close?" | Both answers, with Team Notes as the source. It cost 27 credits |
