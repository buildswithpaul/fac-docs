# Guide me: choose what your home page shows

A new chat opens on your home page. Its **Needs you** cards come from your own ERPNext: approvals waiting for you, invoices overdue, orders running late. Each card counts only what you can open, and it is built for your role.

## 1. Look at what needs you

Open **New chat**. Click a card's link to ask FACO the exact question behind it.

![The home page with three Needs you cards](/img/guide-me/chat-home.webp)

## 2. Hide a card you do not need

Click **⋯** on the card and choose **Don't show this on my home**. The next card moves up.

![The card menu with "Don't show this on my home"](/img/guide-me/home-card-menu.webp)

## 3. Choose what appears

Open **Settings → Home page**.

![Settings, Home page](/img/guide-me/settings-home-page.webp)

- **Needs you:** turn the whole section on or off.
- **Suggested questions:** show starter questions.
- **Personalised suggestions:** questions made for you. This is the only part of the page that uses AI, about once a day. Turn it off to save usage.
- **Cards to show:** how many Needs you cards appear at once.
- **Shown in Needs you:** untick anything you do not want. A card you hid from the home page is unticked here, so you can bring it back.

These choices are saved to your account.
