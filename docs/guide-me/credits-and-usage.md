# Guide me: see your credits and usage

FAC Chat is paid for in credits. This page shows what a credit is, where to see how many you have used, and where plans and invoices live.

**You need:** FAC Chat and an up-to-date FAC Cloud. Changing the plan needs the workspace owner or an admin.

## 1. Know what a credit is

Open **Settings → Billing** and expand **What is a credit?** A credit measures how much work a reply took, so a long request costs more than a quick lookup.

![What a credit is](/img/guide-me/billing-credit.webp)

Every answer shows its credits under the message, and the meter beside the model picker shows how much of the month is used.

## 2. See your own usage

Open **Usage** in the left rail and click **Mine**. You see your credits over 7, 30 or 90 days, a daily trend, and a split by source and by model. Admins also get **Workspace**, with usage by user.

![The Usage page for one user](/img/guide-me/usage-mine.webp)

## 3. Manage the plan

The tabs in **Settings → Billing** cover the rest:

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Plans** | Compare plans and change yours, monthly or annual |
| **Credits** | Buy extra credits. They are used once the monthly allowance runs out, and they carry over |
| **Billing Details** | The company name, address and tax details that go on invoices |
| **Payment Method** | The card or mandate used for renewals |
| **Invoices** | Every invoice, with its status |
| **Settings** | Cancel the subscription. It stays active until the end of the paid period |

Seats are added and removed in **Settings → Users**, where an admin can also set a credit limit for each person.

Current prices are on the [pricing page](https://fac-cloud.com/pricing).

## Use cases

| You want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| Find out why this month used more | **Usage → Mine**, then look at the daily trend and the split by model |
| Spend less on routine questions | Leave the model on **Auto**, start a new chat for a new topic, and see [Choose which model answers](./model-routing) |
| Carry on after the monthly allowance runs out | **Billing → Credits → Buy Credits** |
| See who in the team uses the most | **Usage → Workspace** (admins) |
