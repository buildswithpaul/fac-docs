# Guide me: email a CRM lead

FACO writes the email from the lead's history, shows you all of it, and sends it only when you approve.

**You need:** Frappe CRM, with FACO switched on for CRM ([how](./other-frappe-apps)), and an email account you can send from.

## 1. Open the lead and ask

Open the lead in Frappe CRM and click the FACO robot in the corner. Ask for the email in plain words, for example *"Email Priya to propose a demo next Wednesday, and reassure her about moving off Tally"*. FACO reads the lead's history first, so the email can refer to what you have already discussed.

![FACO open on a CRM lead](/img/guide-me/crm-brief.webp)

## 2. Read the email before it goes

FACO shows the email in an approval card: the message in full and the account it **sends from**. Nothing is sent yet.

![The email in an approval card, with Send email and Reject](/img/guide-me/crm-email-card.webp)

Click **Send email** to send it, or **Reject** to stop. If you reject, tell FACO what to change and it drafts again.

## 3. Find it on the lead

The email is sent the way CRM's own composer sends it, so it appears on the lead's **Emails** tab.

![The sent email on the lead's Emails tab](/img/guide-me/crm-emails-tab.webp)

## Also on a lead

- *"I just finished a call with Priya. Log the call, add a note and a follow-up task."* FACO adds each one to the lead, after your approval.
- *"She's qualified. Convert this lead into a deal."* FACO uses CRM's own **Convert to Deal**, so the contact and the history carry over.
